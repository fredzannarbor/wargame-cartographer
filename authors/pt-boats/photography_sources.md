# PT Boat Photography Sources — Acquisition Guide

## Overview

The good news: the vast majority of WWII-era PT boat photographs were taken by U.S. Navy photographers and are **public domain** as works of the United States government. This means they can be published without licensing fees. The challenge is locating, accessing, and obtaining high-resolution scans suitable for book publication (minimum 300 DPI at reproduction size).

---

## Primary Sources

### 1. National Archives and Records Administration (NARA)

**The single largest repository of PT boat photographs.**

- **Collection:** Record Group 80, specifically the **80-G series** (General Records of the Department of the Navy, Bureau of Aeronautics and Office of the Chief of Naval Operations photographic records)
- **Volume:** Thousands of PT boat images scattered throughout the 80-G series
- **Known examples:**
  - 80-G-53855: PTs patrolling off coast of New Guinea, 1943
  - 80-G-58545: USS PT-174, Central Solomon Islands, January 1944
  - 80-G-85754: PT boat No. 285 underway, October 19, 1943
  - 80-G-53830: Morobe PT Boat Base, New Guinea
- **Resolution:** Original negatives can be scanned at very high resolution (publication quality)
- **Access:**
  - **Online:** NARA catalog at catalog.archives.gov; also nara.getarchive.net for free downloads of digitized holdings
  - **In person:** Still Picture Branch, NARA at College Park, 8601 Adelphi Road, College Park, MD 20740-6001
  - **Contact:** stillpix@nara.gov, phone 301-837-0561
  - **Reproduction:** Digital scans can be ordered; prices vary by format and priority
- **Rights:** Public domain. No attribution legally required, though crediting "National Archives" is standard practice.
- **Tip:** The 80-G series is indexed numerically and can be browsed in blocks of 10,000 via the NHHC website. Not all images are digitized—the physical collection is vastly larger than what's online.

### 2. Naval History and Heritage Command (NHHC)

**The Navy's own historical photo archive.**

- **Collection:** Over 1 million photographs from mid-19th century to 1990s. PT boat images in both the 80-G series (shared with NARA) and the **NH series** (NHHC's own collection)
- **Access:**
  - **Online:** history.navy.mil/our-collections/photography.html — searchable, downloadable in low/medium/high resolution
  - **In person:** By appointment only, Washington Navy Yard
  - **Contact:** NHHC-Photo@us.navy.mil, phone 202-433-7881
  - **Limit:** 5 scans per month per requester for special requests
- **Rights:** Majority public domain. NH-series images should be credited: "NH [number] courtesy of the Naval History & Heritage Command." Some privately donated images may have restrictions—check individual image copyright notes.
- **Tip:** NHHC has curated photo essays on various naval topics; check for any PT-specific collections.

### 3. Library of Congress — Prints & Photographs Division

- **Collection:** 14+ million items, 1+ million searchable online. PT boat photos found in the "Selected Pictures of World War II Battles" collection (official Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps photographer output)
- **Access:**
  - **Online:** Prints & Photographs Online Catalog (PPOC) at loc.gov/pictures/ — search "PT boat" or "patrol torpedo"
  - **Contact:** Prints and Photographs Reading Room, 202-707-6394, or Ask A Librarian service
- **Rights:** "Selected Pictures of WWII Battles" collection: "no known restrictions on publication." Check individual catalog entries.
- **Tip:** Not a primary PT boat source, but useful for supplementary images and images not duplicated elsewhere.

### 4. PT Boats, Inc. (Germantown, Tennessee)

**The world's largest dedicated PT boat archive.**

- **Collection:** Approximately **11,000 photographs** plus manuals, charts, logs, diaries, film, factory blueprints, and 100+ books
- **Founded:** 1967 by James "Boats" Newberry (WWII PT veteran, RON 9)
- **Locations:**
  - **Headquarters/Archives:** 1384 Cordova Cv, #2, Germantown, TN 38138; phone 901-755-8440; open daily 9:00am–4:30pm
  - **Museum & Library:** Battleship Cove, Fall River, Massachusetts
- **Access:** Visit in person or contact via ptboats.org
- **Rights:** **Mixed.** Navy-origin photos in their collection are public domain regardless of who holds them. Photos taken privately by veterans or donated by families may have different rights. Determine provenance per image.
- **Tip:** This is THE essential archive for a serious PT boat author. Many images here exist nowhere else—personal snapshots, unofficial photos, crew portraits. Building a relationship with PT Boats Inc. should be a top priority.

## Online Aggregator Sources

### 5. NavSource Naval History — Motor Torpedo Boat Photo Archive

**The most comprehensive online index of individual PT boat photographs.**

- **URL:** navsource.net (Motor Torpedo Boat section)
- **Coverage:** Organized by hull number from PT-1 through PT-810+. Each boat's page aggregates photos from NARA, NHHC, private collections, and other sources with attribution
- **Also hosts:** Downloadable reference documents including "Know Your PT Boat" (1945) and "Motor Torpedo Boats, Tactical Orders and Doctrine" (1942)
- **Rights:** NavSource itself does not hold rights; it indexes and credits sources. Use it as a **finding aid** to locate originals at NARA/NHHC, then obtain publication-quality scans from the original repository.
- **Tip:** Invaluable for identifying which photos exist for specific boats. Start here for research, then go to the source for high-resolution scans.

### 6. Wikimedia Commons

- Extensive collection of NARA/NHHC PT boat photos already uploaded at high resolution
- All clearly marked as public domain
- Good for quick reference; verify against NARA originals for publication-quality versions
- Search: commons.wikimedia.org, keywords "PT boat," "motor torpedo boat," specific hull numbers

### 7. Fold3 (Ancestry.com)

- NARA military records digitization partner
- Some PT boat action reports and photos digitized
- Subscription service, but NARA originals can be accessed free at the archives
- Useful for preliminary research before visiting NARA in person

## Theater-Specific Sources

### Mediterranean Theater

- **NARA 80-G series:** Mediterranean subsection contains operational photos from North Africa, Sicily, Italy, Southern France
- **British Imperial War Museum (IWM):** Combined operations photos from Allied Mediterranean campaigns; iwm.org.uk — search their collections online; many images available for licensed publication
- **Italian Navy Historical Archive (Ufficio Storico della Marina Militare):** Axis-perspective photographs of Mediterranean naval operations; access requires correspondence with the Italian Navy
- **French Naval Archives (Service Historique de la Défense, Vincennes):** Operation Dragoon/Southern France photos

### Pacific Theater

- **NARA 80-G series:** Extensive Pacific coverage
- **Australian War Memorial (AWM):** PT-Australian Army cooperation photos; awm.gov.au — excellent online collection, many images downloadable
- **MacArthur Memorial Archive (Norfolk, VA):** Philippines campaign photos

### Construction/Industrial

- **National WWII Museum (New Orleans):** Higgins Industries collection — factory floor photos, construction documentation, Andrew Higgins personal papers
- **Mystic Seaport Museum:** Early small craft and yacht design records relevant to pre-war PT development

## Practical Acquisition Strategy

### For Each Book

1. **Research phase:** Use NavSource and NHHC online catalogs to identify relevant images by subject and 80-G/NH number
2. **Selection:** Create a shot list of 80–100 candidate images (for a final selection of 30–60)
3. **Acquisition:**
   - Download any available high-resolution digital files from NHHC website (free)
   - Order custom scans from NARA Still Picture Branch for images only available as physical negatives/prints
   - Visit PT Boats Inc. archive for unique images not available elsewhere
   - Contact IWM/AWM/foreign archives for theater-specific supplementary images
4. **Rights clearance:** Document provenance for every image. Navy/government photos = public domain. Private/donated photos = obtain written permission from rights holder.
5. **Caption research:** Every photo should have a detailed caption including date, location, boat number(s), and personnel identified where possible.

### Cost Estimates

- NHHC digital downloads: **Free**
- NARA custom scans: **$15–$80 per image** depending on format and priority
- PT Boats Inc.: **Negotiable** — likely modest reproduction fees for nonprofit
- IWM licensed images: **£30–£100+ per image** depending on use
- AWM images: **Many free for publication** with credit; check individual image terms

### Budget per Book (Photography)

- Estimated 50–60 images per book
- Mix of free downloads (60%) and paid scans (40%)
- **Estimated cost: $500–$1,500 per book** for image acquisition
- Higher for books requiring foreign archive access (Mediterranean, Philippines)
